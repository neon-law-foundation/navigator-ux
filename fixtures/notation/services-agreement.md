# Services Agreement

This Services Agreement is made between the Provider and the Client, each a
party, as of the {{Effective Date}}.

## I. Services

The Provider will perform the services described in each order form the
parties sign under this agreement.

1. The Provider assigns qualified personnel to each order form and keeps the
   Client informed of any change in the people assigned;
2. the Client gives the Provider timely access to the information, systems,
   and decision-makers the services need;
3. each party names one contact for day-to-day coordination; and
4. either party may propose a change to an order form in writing, and a change
   binds only once both parties sign it.

## II. Fees and Payment

The Client pays the fees stated in each order form.

1. The Provider invoices monthly in arrears, and each invoice itemizes the
   services performed in the month it covers;
2. the Client pays each undisputed invoice within thirty days of receipt; and
3. a disputed amount is raised in writing within fifteen days, with enough
   detail for the Provider to respond.

## III. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential and uses it
only to perform this agreement.

- Confidential information excludes what is public through no fault of the
  receiving party.
- A party may disclose what the law requires, after notice to the other party
  where the law allows it.

## IV. Term and Termination

This agreement runs for [TERM] from the Effective Date.

1. Either party may end this agreement on sixty days' written notice;
2. either party may end it at once if the other materially breaches it and
   does not cure the breach within thirty days of notice; and
3. sections III and IV survive the end of this agreement.
